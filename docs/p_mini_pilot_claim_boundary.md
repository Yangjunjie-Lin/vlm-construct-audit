# P Mini-Pilot Claim Boundary

Allowed central terms are `behavioral intervention effect`, `behavioral claim certification`,
`scope-bounded effect`, and `claim eligibility`.

An eligible positive result may support only a statement that correct rather than corrupted
supplied evidence changed top-ranked candidate-answer correctness by a certified amount under the
frozen protocol. An audit-value result may support only that P3 changed the permitted scope of a
behavioral-effect claim relative to naive reporting.

The following statements are prohibited:

- the model used an identified internal reasoning mechanism;
- the model possessed an internal scene graph;
- internal relation composition occurred;
- P3 identified a causal pathway inside the model;
- a naive conclusion was proved wrong as an internal-mechanism claim;
- the result generalizes to all VLMs, models, images, serializations, or answer contracts.

This study does not establish answer-contract invariance. Free generation, grammar-constrained
generation, prompt-only JSON, semantic repair, fuzzy parsing, and LLM-as-judge scoring are not
primary endpoints.
