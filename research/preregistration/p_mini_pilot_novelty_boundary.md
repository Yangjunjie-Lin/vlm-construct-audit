# Direction P Mini-Pilot Novelty Boundary

Cutoff: **2026-08-30**. Decision: **NOVELTY_PASS_WITH_CAUTION**.

The search updated the 2026-08-27 Post-STOP audit through the execution date using official
arXiv paper/search pages and publisher pages for primary sources. It covered VLM selective
prediction, multimodal conformal/risk control, SESOI and minimum-effect power, behavioral
interventions, construct-valid evaluation, false-mechanistic-claim control, claim-level
inference, and selective inference for treatment effects. The update is systematic but not proof
that no unindexed or unpublished collision exists.

## Nearest work and collision decision

The closest VLM neighbors are Yu, Niu & He's answer-level trust selection across 20 VLM
backbones; Xu et al.'s budgeted conformal evidence acquisition; and Li & Liu's EviSafe
counterfactual-evidence evaluation across eleven VLMs. Kotte is the closest feasibility and
impossibility analysis for conformal certification of structured language-model outputs.
Riesthuis and Lakens establish the SESOI-aware power and equivalence ingredients.

No located paper simultaneously performs all of the following: a VLM evidence intervention,
known-state false-claim calibration, fixed SESOI, power-calibrated certification alternative,
claim-level selective/risk-coverage decision, independent intervention-uptake validation, and a
multi-family real open-weight VLM application. Therefore the registered collision rule does not
trigger `NOVELTY_NO_GO`.

## Remaining candidate contribution

The only candidate contribution retained is:

> After known-DGP calibration, prospectively apply a power-calibrated claim-certification
> procedure to scope-bounded supplied-evidence behavioral effects across multiple real
> open-weight VLM families.

The contribution is a joint prospective design and audit target. SESOI, the indifference zone,
minimum-effect testing, selective prediction, conformal risk control, risk-coverage curves,
paired bootstrap, and conditional likelihood are established methods and are not novelty claims.

## Claim boundary

The Mini-Pilot may estimate whether supplied evidence changes observable behavior under the
frozen protocol. It cannot identify an internal reasoning mechanism, establish that a model has
a scene graph, or show a causally verified internal relation-composition pathway. EviSafe and
ATS make especially clear that behaviorally informative interventions do not by themselves
identify internal mechanisms.

The novelty decision must be reopened before inference if a newly located primary source matches
the complete collision conjunction. A collision changes the decision to `NOVELTY_NO_GO`; it does
not justify quietly narrowing the claim after results are seen.
