# Post-STOP Novelty Boundary

Cutoff: 2026-08-27. Primary-source search status: **complete with a non-exhaustiveness
caveat**. This search used OpenAlex, arXiv, and Crossref and is evidence against, not proof of the
absence of, a collision.

## Direction decisions

- P: `NOVELTY_PASS_WITH_CAUTION`.
- M: `NOVELTY_PASS_WITH_CAUTION`.
- U: `NOVELTY_PASS`.

No located work jointly matches the VLM setting, known-state calibration, false
mechanistic-claim control, estimand, and decision policy of any direction. Therefore no direction
receives `NOVELTY_NO_GO`, and development may begin under its own preregistration.

## P boundary

The closest works are Answer-Level Trust Selection for physical VLM reasoning, Budgeted Conformal
Evidence Acquisition for hallucination control, and feasibility/impossibility analysis for
conformal certification of structured LLM output. Selective prediction, risk–coverage curves,
conformal risk control, SESOI use, minimum-effect tests, and indifference zones are not new. The
remaining potentially distinct unit is a known-DGP audit of *effect-claim certification* relative
to a fixed scientific SESOI and a power-chosen alternative, while separately rejecting invalid
measurement/intervention states and measuring false mechanistic claims. Even a successful screen
may support only a methodological mini-pilot, not internal-mechanism inference.

## M boundary

Recent primary work already shows that prompt-only structured output can change semantic choices,
that required schema fields can induce fabrication under true grammar constraints, and that format
repair can masquerade as content change. JSONSchemaBench and grammar-constrained-decoding work also
make syntax enforcement an engineering topic, not a scientific novelty claim. M can only retain
scientific potential if a fixed-truth VLM study reveals a reproducible, cross-family semantic
contract effect after parser rejection and serialization are separated. Contract equivalence is
valuable infrastructure but is not a Q1 central contribution by itself.

## U boundary

Principal stratification, IV/CACE, post-treatment bias, weak-instrument warnings, and partial
identification are established causal tools. Recent work studies false-claim adoption in a
text-only synthetic task and counterfactual evidence sensitivity in VLM safety, but no located
study calibrates encouragement-based VLM uptake estimands against known potential states while
requiring correct point/partial/non-identification decisions and false-mechanistic-claim control.
That joint calibration, not the causal vocabulary, is the only prospective novelty.

## Prohibited novelty claims

The following are never sufficient novelty: using SESOI, adding a gray zone, conformal prediction,
constrained decoding, JSON Schema, principal stratification, an instrumental variable,
partial-identification bounds, prompt comparison, or exact-versus-semantic parsing. None of the
three screens may claim accurate identification of an internal VLM mechanism.
