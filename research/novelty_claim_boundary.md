# Novelty Claim Boundary

Cutoff: 2026-08-26. Status: **PASS WITH CAUTION** for Tier 0 implementation.

The bounded primary-source audit found no work that simultaneously combines known-state
mechanism calibration, intervention validity, measurement-contract validity, false
mechanistic-claim control, and multi-family VLM evaluation. This is not proof that no such
work exists.

The closest benchmark neighbour is SugarCrepe; the closest false-mechanistic-success
analysis is Sutter et al.; the closest VLM mechanistic benchmark is MMIB; the theoretical
measurement foundation is Jacobs and Wallach; and the causal handling of post-treatment
uptake follows principal stratification and partial-identification work.

## Potential contribution

The only presently defensible contribution is the joint, executable calibration of a
validity-aware behavioral-claim audit against known true and false systems, including its
false-claim rate, sensitivity, coverage, Type-S/M error, and abstention. The contribution is
not any individual ingredient and is provisional until a three-family VLM pilot is run.

## Claims not novel or not permitted

- VLMs are prompt- or format-sensitive.
- MCQA is affected by option order or surface form.
- Synthetic VQA, matched foils, causal tracing, attribute binding, or cross-modal conflict
  are new problem settings.
- The audit identifies an internal mechanism from accuracy.
- NL-to-triples equivalence alone is a scientific contribution.
- A provisional project acronym is a validated method brand.

MMIB raises the strongest active collision risk. Its official abstract reports two VLMs,
modality-specific counterfactuals, circuit metrics, and interchange-intervention accuracy.
The proposed audit must demonstrate distinct value through known-state behavioral systems,
measurement/uptake separation, post-treatment-safe estimands, and quantified false-claim
control. If it reduces to ordinary prompt sensitivity or manipulation checking, novelty fails.
