# Final claim boundary

Decision: `TERMINATE_SUCCESSOR_PROGRAM`. This is a negative-evidence and research-governance
closeout. It is not a successful method, a leaderboard, a completed benchmark, or authority to
write a claim-bearing paper.

## Allowed claims

1. ReCoAlign is a frozen negative-evidence case.
2. Downstream accuracy alone cannot identify internal VLM mechanisms.
3. In the known-DGP evaluation, P3 reduced the preregistered false-claim rate.
4. The v1 preregistration was rejected by independent audit because of construct leakage.
5. The v2 automated construct checks passed.
6. V2 did not obtain a human construct-review result that can authorize scientific execution.
7. The successor program executed no formal VLM scientific pilot.
8. The process shows how strict research gates can prevent unsupported mechanism claims.

The P result in item 3 is controlled calibration evidence only. It cannot support a real-VLM
effect or internal-mechanism claim. The automated pass in item 5 establishes consistency with the
frozen automated checks; it cannot override a failed or integrity-inconclusive human gate. A CI
pass establishes that software and preserved records agree with their tests, not that a scientific
claim is true.

## Prohibited claims

1. P3 has been validated on real VLMs.
2. V2 proves that VLMs can or cannot perform bridge composition.
3. The human review proves that the v2 genuine construct is invalid.
4. A missed decoy proves that either reviewer was dishonest.
5. VGCA has achieved a Q1-level method contribution.
6. VLM-CVA is a completed community benchmark.
7. Multi-model internal-mechanism conclusions have been produced.
8. The current project may enter claim-bearing paper writing.

No real-VLM Direction P scientific result exists. Genuine items all passed their required fields,
but each reviewer detected only 12/16 valid decoys and the preserved artifacts do not credibly
establish reviewer independence. This combination terminates Direction P; it neither validates nor
falsifies bridge-composition ability.
